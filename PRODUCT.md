# Sports Training Hub

## Product truth

Sports Training Hub is a lightweight planning board for organising training
sessions by focus, duration, and intensity. It helps a person choose a session
and see its structure; it is not medical, injury, or performance advice.

## First release

- Filter a small library of sessions by focus and intensity.
- Select a session to see its warm-up, work, and recovery blocks.
- Mark a session as queued for the week in the local browser.
- Keep effort guidance descriptive rather than prescriptive.

## Evidence boundary

Session content is a self-authored demonstration dataset. It is not reviewed
by a coach or clinician and should be adapted to the user's circumstances.
